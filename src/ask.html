How this works

Every question above maps to a short list of passages chosen by hand — the same references a printed topical index or a pastor would give you. Nothing here is generated by a machine, so nothing can be invented. A confident wrong answer about scripture would be worse than no answer at all.

The text itself comes from getBible, which serves freely-licensed translations at no cost and without an account. When your language is not English, the English text is shown beside it — useful if you are learning English, and useful if you grew up with the English words and want to read them in your first language.

Want to read further? The online Bible has all 66 books in every language listed here, with the same side-by-side view. A question can also be linked directly — ask.html?topic=grief — so a sermon page or a pastoral email can point straight at the right passages.
