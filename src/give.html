Give

An expression of our worship

“Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver.” — 2 Corinthians 9:7

How much you give is something you should work out with God, but we believe that our giving expresses something of our worship to God. Your offerings help to fulfil the vision we believe God has given us. They allow us to run programmes and services for the church and in our wider community, and they support people around New Zealand and overseas involved in missional activities.

Visiting? You are not expected to give

Giving is something our church family does. Nothing is passed along the rows, and nobody will ask you. This page is here for when you want it, not before.

How to give

Set up an automatic payment or give by online banking. The account details are on the church’s live giving page — on the real site they belong here, with a copy button beside the account number, because typing a bank account off a screen is exactly where mistakes happen.

Tax receipts

If you want your giving recorded for tax receipt purposes, just make sure your name is in the reference field on bank deposits.

Who knows how much I give?

Our pastors and leadership teams are not involved in any way with our giving records. Your information is confidential to our administration team.
