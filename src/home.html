Who we are

Loving God wholeheartedly, connecting together intentionally, serving others boldly

We love the many nationalities who call New Zealand home, and we are working towards a more inter-cultural community where we learn from our differences and are strengthened by our diversity.

We are an intergenerational, multicultural church in Hillcrest, on Auckland’s North Shore. We preach from God’s Word, we strive to do what it says, we rest and listen to the Spirit, and we go out and share this love with others. Our history is bathed in mission, and we love supporting family in their work overseas.
