Northcote Baptist Church

Nau mai, haere mai

Welcome to Northcote Baptist Church in Hillcrest, on Auckland’s North Shore. We are an intergenerational, multicultural church, and our values are ones we already name in te reo: Aroha, Whanaungatanga, Manaakitanga and Pono — love, connection, service and truth.

Te karakia o te Rātapu · Our Sunday service

About an hour and a quarter: a mix of traditional and contemporary worship songs, prayer, and a message from the Bible. Communion is shared on the first Sunday of the month. Everyone is welcome, whoever you are and wherever you are from.

Ngā tamariki · Your children

Children begin the service with the adults and then go out to their own programmes, which run during school term time. There is a dedicated space for parents with babies, with a kitchenette and a live video feed of the service.

Te Paipera Tapu · The Bible in te reo

Our online Bible includes Te Paipera Tapu, and can show it beside an English translation, verse by verse. It is free to use and needs no account.

Whakapā mai · Get in touch

If you would like someone to meet you at the door on your first Sunday, email office@nbc.org.nz and we will arrange it.
