What the service is like

Our services are family friendly, with a mix of traditional and contemporary worship songs, prayer and a message. We have communion on the first Sunday of the month, and we love to give updates on what God is doing in our church family, in our local community, and through our overseas workers.

Children

During term time we have a variety of children’s programmes during the service, from pre-school up to Year 9. Children start the service with the adults before heading out. During the school holidays and over long weekends we have activity packs available instead.

Babies

We have a dedicated Mum and Baby space with a kitchenette and live video of the service, so you can step out without missing anything.

Afterwards

After the service, please stay around for morning tea — we would love to get to know you. If you would rather slip away quietly, that is completely fine too.

During the week

Want more of church during the week? Join a Home Group — it is far easier to get to know people in a small group than in a crowd.
